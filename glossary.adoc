Active Voice::
  A grammatical voice in which the subject of the sentence performs the action expressed by the verb.
  For example, "The operator records the measurement."

Adjective::
  A word that indicates an attribute or quality of a noun (e.g., "red," "quick," "large").

Adverb::
    A word that modifies a verb, adjective, prepositional phrase, clause, sentence, or other adverb, often indicating manner, place, time, or degree (e.g., "quickly," "very," "here").
